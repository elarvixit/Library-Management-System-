# Library Assistant: questions and answers

The **Library Assistant** is the 🤖 button in the top bar (keyboard shortcut **A**). It is a built-in help assistant: it runs in the browser, needs no API key, and answers from the library's **live data**. It picks up member names, member IDs, book titles and ISBNs typed in a question.

It **only reads**. Answers come with shortcut buttons (open member, collect fine, issue, and so on), and every action still goes through the normal screens and confirmations.

Each question type accepts many wordings and works with **any** member or book in the library. For example, "Can Ravi borrow Dune?" works equally for "Is Priya allowed to take Harry Potter?". The answers below were generated by the assistant itself from the demo data (`npm run seed`). On your library, the names and numbers will be your own.

## Library rules

**30. How are fines calculated?** (also "What is the fine per day?")

> **Late fines** are **₹5 per day** after the due date.
> - Returning on the due date is not late.
> - Example: 4 days late = 4 × ₹5 = **₹20**.
> - The fine is calculated and recorded against the member when the book is returned.
> - Unpaid fines **block new issues and renewals** until the librarian clicks Collect.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open fines ledger`

**31. How many books can a member borrow?**

> A member can hold **at most 3 books** at a time. To borrow another, they must return one first.

**32. How long is the loan period?**

> The default loan period is **14 days** from the issue date. On the Issue screen you can pick 1–4 weeks or set any due date (not before the issue date).
>
> _Buttons:_ `Issue a book`

**33. Renewal rules**

> **Renewing** extends a loan to **today + 14 days**, up to **2 times**. It's refused when:
> - the book is already overdue (it must be returned and the fine paid)
> - the member has unpaid fines
> - someone is waiting in the reservation queue for that book
>
> Use the **Renew** button on the Return Book page. If it's disabled, hover it to see why.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open loans`

**34. How do reservations work?**

> **Reservations** work like this:
> - A member can reserve a book only when **no copy is on the shelf**.
> - The queue is **first come, first served**.
> - When a copy is returned it goes to the **first person in the queue**, appears on Ready for pickup, and is **held for 2 days**.
> - While people are waiting, the book can only be issued to the first person in the queue.
> - If the hold expires, the copy passes to the next person (or back to the shelf).
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open reservations`

**35. What happens when a hold expires?**

> A copy **ready for pickup** is held for **2 days**.
> - The member can collect it any time until the "held until" date (inclusive).
> - From the next day the hold **expires**: the copy passes to the next person in the queue, with a fresh 2-day hold.
> - If nobody else is waiting, the copy goes back on the shelf for anyone to borrow.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open reservations`

**36. Can I delete a book that is issued?**

> A book **cannot be deleted while any copy is on loan** or while it has open reservations — the app refuses and tells you who has it. Deleting keeps past loans and fines in the records.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open books`

**37. What happens to inactive members?**

> Inactive members **cannot borrow or reserve**. Making a member inactive cancels their open reservations and passes any held copy to the next person in the queue. They must still return books they already have.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open members`

## How-to guides

**38. How do I issue a book?**

> To **issue a book**:
> 1. Open **Issue Book** in the sidebar (or press `I`).
> 2. Search and select the **member**.
> 3. Search and select the **book**.
> 4. Check the due date (default 14 days) and the rule checklist on the right.
> 5. Click **Issue book** — you can print a loan slip afterwards.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Go to Issue`

**39. How do I return a book?**

> To **return a book**:
> 1. Open **Return Book** in the sidebar (or press `R`).
> 2. Find the loan by member, title or ISBN.
> 3. Click **Return** and confirm the return date — any late fine is shown before you confirm.
> 4. Print the receipt or collect the fine straight away.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Go to Returns`

**40. How do I reserve a book?**

> To **reserve a book**:
> 1. Go to **Reservations**.
> 2. Select the member and the book (only books with no copy on the shelf are listed).
> 3. Click **Reserve** — the member joins the end of the queue.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open reservations`

**41. How do I renew a loan?**

> To **renew a loan**:
> 1. Open **Return Book** in the sidebar.
> 2. Click **Renew** on the loan.
> 3. If the button is disabled, hover it to see why (overdue, unpaid fines, or someone waiting).
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open loans`

**42. How do I import books?**

> To **import books from CSV**:
> 1. Go to **Books** and click **Template** to download an example file.
> 2. Fill in title, author, isbn, category, total_copies.
> 3. Click **Import CSV**, check the preview, then confirm.
> 4. Rows with errors (missing fields, bad or duplicate ISBN) are skipped and listed.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open books`

**43. How do I export data?**

> To **export data**:
> - **Overdue list**: Return Book → Overdue CSV.
> - **Books / Members**: the Export button on each page.
> - **Fines**: Fines → Export CSV.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Download overdue CSV`

**44. How do I add a book?**

> To **add a book**:
> 1. Go to **Books** and click **Add book**.
> 2. Enter title, author, ISBN (10 or 13 digits), category and total copies.
> 3. Click **Add book** — all copies start on the shelf.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Add a book`

**45. How do I add a member?**

> To **add a member**:
> 1. Go to **Members** and click **Add member**.
> 2. Enter the name (member ID is generated if left blank), phone and email.
> 3. Click **Add member** — you can print a library card straight away.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Add a member`

**46. How do I edit a member?**

> To **edit a member**:
> 1. Open **Members** in the sidebar.
> 2. Click the pencil icon on the member (or open them and click **Edit**).
> 3. Change name, phone, email or active status and click **Save changes**.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open members`

**47. How do I edit a book?**

> To **edit a book**:
> 1. Open **Books** in the sidebar.
> 2. Click the pencil icon on the book (or open it and click **Edit**).
> 3. Change the details or total copies and click **Save changes**. Extra copies go to the reservation queue first.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open books`

**48. How do I delete a book?**

> To **delete a book**:
> 1. Open **Books** and click the bin icon on the book (or open it and click **Delete**).
> 2. It is refused while any copy is on loan or reserved — return or cancel those first.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open books`

**49. How do I collect a fine?**

> To **collect a fine**:
> 1. Open **Fines** (or the member's profile).
> 2. Click **Collect** next to the member.
> 3. Confirm — all their unpaid fines are cleared and they can borrow again.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open fines`

**50. How do I print a library card?**

> To **print a card, slip or receipt**:
> - **Loan slip**: shown right after issuing a book.
> - **Return receipt**: shown right after a return.
> - **Library card**: open a member and click **Library card**.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open members`

**51. How do I search?**

> To **search**:
> - Press `Ctrl` + `K` anywhere to search books, members and actions.
> - Press `/` to jump to the search box on the current page.
> - On **Books** you can search by title, author, ISBN or category and filter to available only.
>
> _Buttons:_ `Open search`

## Questions it cannot answer

It is not a general AI chatbot, so questions outside the library get a polite reply and suggestions.

**52. asdfgh** and **53. What is the weather?**

> Sorry, I didn't understand that. I'm a built-in help assistant, so I work best with questions like the suggestions below — mention a **member's name** or a **book title** and I'll look them up.

When a question contains a word that matches a book or member but the intent is unclear, the assistant offers matching books and members as buttons instead.
